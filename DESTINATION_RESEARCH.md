# Maqka Summit — Destination Research Catalogue

This document works through STEPS 1–3 of the research workflow: it structures the 32
candidate destinations, separates well-established general facts from anything that
needs verification, and converts the useful background into original Maqka Summit
wording. **No text below is copied from Tripadvisor** — descriptions are written fresh,
and no traveler reviews are referenced or treated as Maqka Summit testimonials.

**Nothing in this document has been added to the live site.** Per the brief, destinations
are not auto-published — this is the reference material for Maqka Summit to review,
correct, and hand back with official prices before anything goes live. The `category`
and `county` fields now exist in the database and admin dashboard, so any of these can be
entered directly once approved.

## How to read each entry
- **Status**: `Well-established` (general geographic/public fact, safe to state) or
  `Needs verification` (specific numbers/rules that must be confirmed before publishing)
- Anything not listed as a fact is intentionally left blank rather than invented —
  Maqka Summit should fill in price, exact distance, official fees, and opening hours

### 1. Karura Forest
- **Category:** Forest · **County:** Nairobi
- **Status:** Well-established — an urban forest reserve in Nairobi with a network of
  marked walking/running trails, a waterfall, and cave sites open to the public.
- **Needs verification:** current entry fee, exact trail distances, opening hours, whether
  a ranger/guide is required for all routes.
- **Original short description:** A forested escape inside Nairobi's city limits, with
  shaded trails, a waterfall stop, and an easy pace that suits almost any fitness level.
- **Suitable for:** Beginners, families, groups · **Trip format:** Half day

### 2. Ngong Hills
- **Category:** Hill Range · **County:** Kajiado
- **Status:** Well-established — already an active Maqka Summit adventure
  (`ngong-hills-ridge-walk`). Ridge walk over a series of connected hilltops with
  Rift Valley views on one side and Nairobi on the other.
- **Needs verification:** none outstanding beyond current listing.

### 3. Oloolua Nature Trail
- **Category:** Nature Trail · **County:** Kajiado (Karen/Ngong area)
- **Status:** Well-established — a forested trail system near Karen known for caves and
  a waterfall along the route.
- **Needs verification:** entry fee, trail length, current access/operating status.
- **Original short description:** A quiet forest walk on Nairobi's edge, following a
  stream down to a small waterfall and a cave stop along the way.
- **Suitable for:** Beginners, families · **Trip format:** Half day

### 4. Bamburi Nature Trail

- **Needs verification:** entry fee, trail length, current facilities.

### 5. Mount Kenya

### 6. Mount Satima (Aberdare Range)
- **Category:** Mountain Range · **County:** Nyandarua
- **Status:** Well-established — the highest peak in the Aberdare Range; Maqka Summit

  as the official listing before adding a second, separate Aberdare product.
- **Needs verification:** exact summit elevation as currently signed on-site, route access.

### 7. Torok Waterfalls

- **Original short description:** A dramatic waterfall hike down into the Kerio Valley

- **Suitable for:** Intermediate/experienced hikers · **Trip format:** Full day

### 25. Mount Elgon

- **Original short description:** A big, multi-day volcano trek in western Kenya, with

- **Suitable for:** Experienced hikers · **Trip format:** Multi-day
